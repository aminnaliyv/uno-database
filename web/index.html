Color Match

Match the top card by color or value. First to empty their hand wins.

Players
Rules
  • Each player starts with 5 cards.
  • Play a card that matches the top card's color or value.
  • Can't (or don't want to) play? Click the draw pile — you take one card and your turn ends.
  • Skip skips the next player. Reverse flips the turn direction (acts like Skip with 2 players). Draw Two makes the next player draw 2 and lose their turn.
  • With more than one human, pass the device when asked — your cards stay hidden until you click.
